## Verification notes

- Header-only `--check`: 490/490 GGUF tensors consumed; 538 HF names produced; 333 vision names absent (mmproj); 0 unexpected.
- Round trip on synthetic tensors (forward converter reimplemented from qwen.py, then inverse): reorder, split, merge, conv1d and out_proj are exact; the +1 offset is exact except 2 of 4096 values differing by 2.6e-8 (fp32 precision of the converter's own `+ 1`).
- Real GGUF bytes (layers 0, 3, 4, 56 small tensors fetched by range request): `ssm_a` is strictly negative (consistent with -exp(A_log)); norm tensors centre near 1 (consistent with the +1 offset); `GGUF - 1` for norms and `conv1d` are bf16-representable.
- Preview is NOT bit-identical to the release at bf16 precision. Layer 4 `input_layernorm`: 1844 of 4096 elements differ from GGUF-1 by one bf16 ulp. Layer 4 `A_log`: 2 of 32 elements differ by one ulp from bf16(log(-ssm_a)). `dt_bias` matches bf16 rounding of the GGUF values exactly. The converter writes GGUF-derived values; Preview supplies only names, shapes and dtypes.
- Config: loop (4, 28, 2), mhc (4, 28, streams 2, 20 iterations, fixed_identity False, pre_scale 1.0) and all linear_attn/attention/rope-theta fields agree between GGUF and the Preview config. Differences: GGUF declares YaRN rope (factor 2.0, original 262144) and context 524288; Preview config has neither (applied by default, see --keep-config-rope).
